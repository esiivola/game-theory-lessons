Play first, then formalise

Game theory has a habit of being counterintuitive. Betrayal can be the rational move. Being predictable gets you beaten. A small preference can segregate a whole city. You believe these once you have felt them, so every lesson lets you act before it explains, then hands you the definition and the argument.

1
You commit a guess or a move first. The gap between your guess and what happens is where the learning is.
2
The concrete game comes before the notation. Symbols arrive once the idea is already in your hands.
3
Earlier ideas keep coming back in later quizzes, so they stick instead of fading.
